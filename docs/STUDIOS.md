# Studios, videos and the mini TV

© 2026 James Scott. All rights reserved.

Every scene in Peanut Cam is a studio: the character you opened plus any others you add. Open it with the **Studio** button in the bottom bar.

## Cast

- **Add a character** puts the Peanut, a picture character or a 3D model into the scene beside you.
- Touch a cast member to move it: drag to move, pinch to resize, twist to turn. Tap it for its options: what it does when it isn't on camera, plus size, distance and turn sliders.
- **Lock positions** (Studio panel) stops accidental moves while you perform.

## Loops of you

- **Record a loop of …** records up to 15 seconds of you on the character wearing your face: your eyes and mouth, talking and head movement.
- Whenever that character isn't on camera, it replays the loop, so background characters keep moving and talking.
- Other options for a cast member: **Gentle sway**, **Model's animation** (for 3D models that come with one) or **Still**.
- Loops belong to the character, so they play in any studio that character is cast in.

## Cameras

- **Close-up**, **Medium** and **Wide** in the Studio panel add a camera. The camera icon on a cast row (or **Put on camera**) adds one framed on that character.
- The numbered buttons on the left switch cameras; the top button returns to the main view.
- **Your face goes to whoever the camera is on.** The character it leaves goes back to its loop.
- Long-press a camera button to adjust its view (one finger orbits, two fingers move and zoom), change who it's on, or delete it.
- Cameras on a character follow that character when you move it. Wide cameras stay where they are.
- **Smooth camera moves** glides between cameras; turn it off for hard cuts.

## Videos

- **Background › Video** plays a playlist behind your character. **Loop one video** repeats the current clip; **Play all in order, loop** plays the list and starts again.
- **Settings › Look › Add a TV** puts a TV in the scene with its own playlist, so your character can watch and react. Tap the TV for its controls: a seek bar, play/pause, next/previous, **Camera on TV**, volume, the list and **TV settings**.
- **TV settings:**
  - **Model:** Retro, Flat screen, Big CRT (curved glass, scanlines) or Computer monitor.
  - **Size and angle:** size up to 8×, tilt (tip it forward or back), lean and turn.
  - **Always in front** (on by default) draws the TV over everything, so no character blocks it, and keeps it sharp in the PS1/PS2 styles.
  - **Picture:** Fit or Fill, **Trim black bars automatically**, zoom, sharpness, brightness, contrast, colour and CRT scanlines.
  - **Blurred video behind the scene:** while the TV plays, a soft, dimmed copy fills the background, in recordings and streams too.
  - **Playback speed:** 0.5× to 2×.
- **Camera on TV** frames the screen so viewers can see what's playing; your face stays where it is. It follows the TV's model, size and angle unless you adjust it by hand.
- Headset buttons and lock-screen controls can play, pause, skip and seek the TV where the browser supports them (Media Session).
- Video sound is included in recordings and live streams. **Hear video sound on this phone** turns off only the phone's speaker. Wear headphones when streaming with the microphone on, so the mic doesn't pick the video up a second time.

## Placing a face on a 3D model

**Settings › Face › Place face on model** zooms the camera in on the model's face. Drag each eye (they stay mirrored) and the mouth onto the model, or drag anywhere else to move the whole face. The sliders set face, eye and mouth size. New models open straight into this tool. **Settings › Model › Framing** chooses full body, upper body or head for the normal view.
